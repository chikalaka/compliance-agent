## Field Descriptions

- **Vendor:** Name of the vendor/service provider
- **Product:** Specific product or service being used
- **Type (Usage):** How the product is used (e.g., CRM, Analytics, Infrastructure, Security, etc.)
- **Basis of Processing:** Legal basis for data processing (e.g., Consent, Contract, Legitimate Interest, Legal Obligation)
- **Started Using:** Date when the vendor was onboarded
- **Stopped Using:** Date when the vendor was offboarded (leave blank if still active)
- **Dedicated Service Account/Email:** Email address or account used for the service
- **Confidentiality:** Impact level (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- **Integrity:** Impact level (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- **Stored Sensitive Data:** Yes/No - Does the vendor store sensitive or personal data?
- **Availability:** Impact level (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- **Risk Level:** Overall risk assessment (Low/Medium/High/Critical)
- **Country:** Country where the vendor is based or where data is stored
- **Website:** Vendor's website URL
- **Contact:** Primary contact person or email at the vendor
- **GDPR/Privacy Statement:** Link to vendor's privacy policy or GDPR compliance documentation
- **Security Statement:** Link to vendor's security documentation or certifications
- **Compliance:** Relevant compliance certifications (e.g., SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, HIPAA)
- **SOC-2 Report Reviewed:** Yes/No/N/A - Has the SOC 2 report been reviewed?

## Risk Level Assessment Guide

**Risk Level Calculation:** Consider Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability, and Stored Sensitive Data

- **Low:** Minimal impact if compromised, no sensitive data
- **Medium:** Moderate impact, limited sensitive data
- **High:** Significant impact, contains sensitive data
- **Critical:** Severe impact, critical systems or highly sensitive data

## Review Schedule

- **High/Critical Risk Vendors:** Quarterly review
- **Medium Risk Vendors:** Semi-annual review
- **Low Risk Vendors:** Annual review
