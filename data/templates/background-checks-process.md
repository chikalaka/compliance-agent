# Background Checks Process

In countries where **background checks are legal**, {{COMPANY_NAME}} will conduct a formal background check for all new hires before employment begins.

In countries where **background checks are not legally permitted** (such as **Israel**), {{COMPANY_NAME}} will instead conduct **at least one reference call** with a previous employer or colleague.

During the reference call, we will ask about:

a. The candidate's **professional performance and reliability**

b. The candidate's **teamwork, communication, and integrity**
