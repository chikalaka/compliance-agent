# Access to New Applications Process

**Date:** {{DATE}}

**Owner:** {{OWNER}}

## Purpose

To ensure that employees receive access only to the systems and applications required for their role, following the principle of least privilege.

## Process

### 1. Onboarding

During onboarding, the {{OWNER_TITLE}} reviews the employee's role and determines which applications (e.g., Slack, AWS, GitHub, Linear, Google Workspace) are required. The {{OWNER_TITLE}} or delegated admin grants only the necessary access.

### 2. Requesting Additional Access

If new access is needed later, the employee sends a request via Slack message or email to the {{OWNER_TITLE}}.

### 3. Review

The {{OWNER_TITLE}} reviews the request, ensuring it follows the **least privilege** and **need-to-know** principles.

### 4. Approval and Documentation

If approved, the {{OWNER_TITLE}} grants access and documents the change.
