# Vendor Management Policy – Vendor Termination Process

Owner: {{OWNER}}
Effective Date: {{DATE}}

{{COMPANY}} has implemented a **Vendor Management Policy** that includes detailed procedures for vendor termination to ensure continuity, data security, and compliance with contractual obligations.

**Termination Process:**

1. **Initiation**: A termination process may be initiated by management when a vendor relationship is no longer required, or if performance, compliance, or security issues are identified.

2. **Notification**: The vendor is formally notified of the termination, including the effective date and any contractual offboarding requirements.

3. **Access Revocation**: All vendor accounts, credentials, and integrations (e.g., {{COMPANY_TOOLS}}) are revoked by the {{COMPANY}} {{OWNER}} or authorized personnel.

4. **Data Retrieval and Deletion**: {{COMPANY}} ensures that all company data is securely retrieved from the vendor systems and confirms deletion of any residual data held by the vendor, where applicable.

5. **Documentation**: All termination steps, including data retrieval confirmation and access revocation, are logged and stored in the Vendor Management Tracker.

6. **Review**: The process and documentation are reviewed by the {{OWNER}} to ensure completeness and compliance.

**Policy Review**:
This Vendor Management Policy, including the termination process, is reviewed and approved annually by {{COMPANY}} management to ensure continued alignment with SOC 2 requirements and company operations.
